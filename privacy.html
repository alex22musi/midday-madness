Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

This is the website for Midday Madness, a weekly sports talk show on WIUX 99.1 FM. This policy explains what information this site collects and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Nothing directly. This site has no accounts, no sign-ups, no contact forms, and no comment sections. We do not ask for or store your name, email, or any personal information.
  • Analytics and logs. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may collect standard, anonymized web logs (such as IP addresses and browser type) to operate the site. We do not combine these logs with any other data or use them to identify you.

Third-party services

  • Embedded media. Clips and episodes play from files hosted with this site. If we ever embed third-party players (e.g., YouTube), those services may set their own cookies under their own policies.
  • Outbound links. Links to outside sites (WIUX's live stream, LinkedIn profiles, calendar services) open in new tabs and are governed by those sites' privacy policies.

Cookies

This site does not set any cookies of its own. Third-party services linked or embedded here may use cookies per their own policies.

Children

This site is a general-audience sports talk page. It is not directed at children under 13, and we knowingly collect nothing from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "last updated" date.
