ABOUT THE SHOW

Midday Madness is a weekly sports talk show on WIUX 99.1 FM, covering college football, the NFL, and IU sports — hot takes, debates, and zero chill, every Wednesday at 1 PM.

Alex Musicus

Co-host

Senior sports media student in The Media School at Indiana University, minoring in business at Kelley. He writes weekly college football takes for EnforceTheSport. In spring 2026, he interned at FanDuel Sports Network West, supporting Clippers, Kings, and Angels broadcasts.

Sam Singer

Co-host

Senior media and business student at Indiana University Bloomington. Previously interned at SNY and FanDuel Sports Network, working across sports media production and broadcast operations. He now serves as a sales executive at Campus Collective, connecting brands with college audiences nationwide.
