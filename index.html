ABOUT THE SHOW

Midday Madness is a weekly sports talk show on WIUX 99.1 FM, covering college football, the NFL, and IU sports — hot takes, debates, and zero chill, every Wednesday at 1 PM.

Alex Musicus

Co-host

Sports media student in The Media School at Indiana University, minoring in business at the Kelley School of Business. Spent spring 2026 in Los Angeles through IU’s Semester in LA program as an Office Intern and Production Assistant at FanDuel Sports Network West — supporting game-day production for Clippers, Kings, and Angels broadcasts, from equipment setup to in-studio operations.

Sam Singer

Co-host

Media and business student at Indiana University Bloomington. Previously interned at SNY and FanDuel Sports Network, working across sports media production and broadcast operations. He now serves as a sales executive at Campus Collective, connecting brands with college audiences nationwide.
